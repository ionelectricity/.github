### ⚡ Welcome to iON on GitHub

iON (developed by [Semitrónica](https://semitronica.pt)) provides intelligence and connectivity for residential and commercial energy management. Through our ecosystem—from the ultra-low power [**HANblue®**](https://ionelectricity.com/) processor to our cloud analytics—we help users simulate tariffs, prevent electrical anomalies, and reduce consumption during the most polluting hours.

### 📚 Resources for Developers & Enthusiasts

While our ecosystem offers a plug-and-play experience through the iON App, we provide resources for those who want to build and integrate further:

*   **[Official Documentation](https://dev.ionelectricity.com/)**: Access our docs for integration guides, technical details, and API specifications.
*   **[Community & Forum](https://github.com/orgs/ionelectricity/discussions)**: Join our GitHub Discussions. It is the perfect place to ask for technical help, suggest app features, or share your *smart home* setups.

### 💡 Suggestions and Feedback

If you have an idea to improve the iON app, the HANblue® hardware, or if you found a bug, please share it with us in our **[Discussions Forum](https://github.com/orgs/ionelectricity/discussions)**.
